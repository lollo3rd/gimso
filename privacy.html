Launch list privacy notice

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Who is responsible

Lorenzo Terna (GIMSO) is responsible for GIMSO's launch lists. Contact: gimso3rd@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

When you join a GIMSO launch list through a form (for example a Facebook or Instagram instant form), we collect your email address. We use it only to keep your place on that list and, if the product launches, to send you one email with the launch offer promised in the form. Joining does not make a purchase, start a paid subscription or authorize any payment. We do not add you to an ongoing newsletter and we do not send messages about other products.

Legal basis

Your request: by submitting the form you ask us to send that launch email. You can withdraw this request at any time.

How long we keep it

Until the launch email has been sent, or for 12 months after you sign up, whichever comes first, unless you ask us to remove it earlier. We may keep a minimal record of an unsubscribe request only to respect it.

Who else processes it

Meta processes the form on its platform under its own privacy policy and passes us the email you submit (and technical form and ad identifiers). Access to the list is limited to us and to the service providers needed to store it and send the launch email, acting on our instructions. These providers may process data outside your country. We never sell your email or use it to build advertising audiences.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your email, or to leave a list, at any time: write to gimso3rd@gmail.com with “Unsubscribe”, or use the unsubscribe link in the launch email. If you are in the EU or UK you can also complain to your data protection authority.
